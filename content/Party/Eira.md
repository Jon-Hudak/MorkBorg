[[Eira]] is on the run from the government for blowing up a small naval port in[[ Odinski ]]due to the enslavement of flame elementals to power their ships. Desperate to escape, she fled North to [[Snaefeljoko]] where she met an old contact who informed her the flame elementals were taken from the reserve. She attempted a break in to free them. She managed to sneak in, but the flame elementals were so oppressed they viewed her as an enemy rather than their rescuer. Her help was in vain as they burned her alive.... as she died, she saw that not only were they enslaving them for ships, but planned to use them as bombs as she saw evidence of this before passing on.

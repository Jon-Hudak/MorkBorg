The Goliath set off to the capital to Assassinate [[General Kerstad]] based on a letter that was mysteriously left in front of him. Wanting acceptance from a new government, and never fearing a challenge, he took on the contract. He first scoped the area at the hospital. He went to the Masha hotel to find the general where the letter said. He found several guards in the main hotel, who after a rough fight, he made it to the courtyard. He soon realized the letter was a fake. The general planned to use Ragnar as a symbol to show how to crush a rebellion in single combat. Whether it was spiked wine, or simply the general was that good... Ragnar fell. As he lay dying, he heard an explosion, and the troops were heard rushing to the [[NPCs/The Holy Family/Holy Family]].

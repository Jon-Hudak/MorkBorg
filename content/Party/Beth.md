Arakokra Bard

Beth was on a tour to promote her music with her little companion, so when visiting [[Snjero]] they went to the city of party and music. Her performance was so powerful that it inspired rebellion, and a [[Gogol|rebel leader]], later to be revealed to be the king of [[Jarla]] before the [[Snjero United Nations]] took over, [[Gogol]], took interest. He invited her to talk in the main tent of the[[NPCs/The Holy Family/Holy Family]]. While in the tent, an orcish war ship landed on the shore and stormed the festival. As the snjero guards fell, the rebellion army appeared to counterstrike the orcs. In the fight, [[Gogol]] rescued Beth and [[Lil Beth]], taking them into a rebel transport automated wagon. She managed to miss a cannon shot that seemed destined to kill her. They began to drive off to the rebel base as the resistance fought off the orcs.

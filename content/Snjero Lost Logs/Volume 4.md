This Log tells the tale of [[Beth]] on her adventures with [[Gogol]]: 

Being the only one who survived the attacks, [[Beth]] found herself back in her body in an automated cart heading west with the [[Gogol|hidden Jarla king, Gogol]]. They have just fled the Holy Family Festival and are heading to [[Machina]], the base of the resistance, as [[Gogol]], the hidden king, now leads the resistance group. 

On their way west, they stop at a farm to hook [[Shira|shiras]] (large lizards that pull carts instead of horses due to desert terrain) to the cart to disguise it as a normal cart. In return for the [[Shira|shiras]], the farmer asks the party to take care of some desert spiders. [[Beth]] befriends a cat named [[Dede]] who joins them, and it says "MUSHROOMS" 

The party finds the desert spider den, and use it as an opportunity to see how well [[Beth]] fights. [[Gogol]] assists her and they head to the city of [[Merdan]], which is the last city that the train stops. 

As they travel to [[Merdan]], [[Gogol]] questions [[Beth]] on what happened when she entered a trancelike sleep. She reveals that the [[NPCs/The Holy Family/Holy Family]] has asked her to meet the three who were resurrected at the [[Machina]], to gain the rebels aid on who destroyed the temples. [[Gogol]], being a figurehead of the resistance, reveals that he was heading to the [[Machina]], and the best option would be to wait for her party in [[Merdan]] so they could travel to the [[Machina]] together, as the desert is very dangerous west of [[Merdan]]. [[Beth]] agrees to this and has a day to explore [[Merdan]]. 

She first goes to the market, and seeing that street performing is common, she freestyles, gaining a lot of coin to spend in the market. She buys a number of interesting goods, and gets into a fist fight with [[Moshi]], a shop owner, for ripping her off. All ends well though as fights are common in the [[Merdan]] market. She performs again earning more coin, buying more things, and leaves the market with quite a haul! 

She makes her way to the local temple where she tells the head of the temple that she is on her way to the [[Machina]] to meet the rebels. He is uncomfortable hearing the base location, and recommends she stop at the tavern as the emperor will be making an announcement on the attacks soon.

She does so, and arrives early, finding a bard playing, who she challenges to a duel! Turns out, the bard is [[Gogol]]! Gogol turned out to be a musician to hide his true identity in his youth, and takes the challenge with joy! She beats him, and the announcement of the emperor comes in the radio... the announcement and the rebel theme playing right after causes unrest, which Gogol then urges her to lay low in her hotel room. 

She goes to her room, and before she sleeps, she tries an unusual item she found in the market, a piece of weird cheese. Upon eating it, she is transported to another dimension, where she is a human at a shopping mall! Before dying in the alternate dimension, she gains a sword and armor! Wild!!! 

She awakens back in her body, and with [[Lil Beth]] and [[Dede]], she goes to bed in her room after a long and exhausting day. Based on what [[Gogol]] said, her companions will have to pass through [[Merdan]] to get to the [[Machina]], so she will likely be meeting them in the flesh very soon...

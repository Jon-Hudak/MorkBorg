[[Ragnar]] is somewhere between living and dead, as his soul is slipping between the afterlife of the[[ Holy Family]] and the [[Tiefling Gods]].. the first voice he hears is of [[Derrick]], the god of Fear. [[Derrick]] offers him the choice to abandon the [[Holy Family ]]in return for his power,[[ Ragnar]] accepts, and gains the ability to call on him for advice once a day. He comes back to life in the [[Black Hand ]]sanctuary yet again, where [[Aleksi]] tells him his party has left. He is instructed that they have gone on the train to their mission at the rebel base. He stops at the tavern for a drink and he will be on his way.

The tavern has more adventurers in it then just [[Ragnar]], however. In the tavern, a stranger going by the name of [[Jaromir]] wants to learn about the rebellion, and a mysterious contact has left him a letter to meet him at the tavern. He manages to find his contact, a man named [[Genrick]]. [[Genrick]] says he will be taking Jaromir to the base. [[Ragnar]], who overhears the two talking, joins them as they are going in the same direction. 

The three board the train and head to [[Merdan]]. The moment they arrive, [[Ragnar]] starts to walk to the base.... realizing as he walks all he sees is sand, he calls upon the aid of [[Derrick]], who insists he should probably get a wagon and [[Shira|shiras]]... he also tells him the location of the base. 

At the same time [[Genrick]] and [[Jaromir]] head to the market, where they find goods, one of them being a strange amulet that [[Genrick]] buys for quite a hefty sum. 

Eventually, the three meet at the [[Shira]] farm where [[Genrick]] has a wagon already purchased, so the three ride out into the desert. Before taking off, [[Genrick]] had an awfully unusual time steering the [[Shira|shiras]]...

As they ride into the desert, they ride straight into a desert spider nest! [[Genrick]] tries to talk to the spiders with no avail, but [[Ragnar]] and a very combat elite [[Jaromir]] manage to kill two and the third flees. The party collects themselves and head back to the wagon. 

As the trio approaches the city of [[Corlah]], [[Ragnar]] and [[Jaromir]] hear a sending stone in [[Genrick]]'s pocket start asking if he has found the rebel base.... oh no.... [[Genrick]] is a member of the secret police!! [[Genrick]]'s cover is blown! He jumps off the wagon and makes a run for it, shooting behind him as [[Ragnar]] and [[Jaromir]] hunt him down! 

As [[Genrick]] is running, not being acquainted with the desert, he falls into a spider pit... [[Ragnar]] and [[Jaromir]] figure him to be dead... however, as he fell to his death, he pulled out the amulet which granted him the ability to call on a random god for help... a certain god of chaos responded and saved him, unbeknownst to the party... 

With the traitor dead, [[Ragnar]] and [[Jaromir]] take their wagon, and being close to [[Corlah]], they ride off to the city to take rest before heading to the base.

The party is split, [[Beth]] is in [[Merdan]] while the rest of the party is in [[Burgen]]. 

In [[Merdan]], [[Beth]], along with [[Gogol]] and her little friends wait for the rest of her party as passing through [[Merdan]] appears to be the only way to get to the rebel base. While in [[Merdan]], [[Beth]] makes some more money and beats up a shopkeep, who ended up using the publicity to make money! 

[[Eira]], [[Viktor]] and [[Ragnar]] all arrive at the [[Black Hand]] sanctuary at once, however, [[Ragnar]] finds himself weak, as he has now interacted with the tiefling pantheon, right after being reborn by the [[NPCs/The Holy Family/Holy Family]], he falls unconscious, possibly dead. [[Eira]] and [[Viktor]] decide they must carry on without him, while the rest of the [[Black Hand]] will be looking into what is going on with this looming vampire threat. They take the train to [[Merdan]], the last stop on the line that will get them as close as possible to the rebel base. 

[[Gogol]]'s prediction was spot on, as [[Beth]], [[Ragnar]], and [[Viktor]] meet in the [[Merdan]] Market. [[Gogol]] then takes this gathered party in his wagon (running on [[shira|shiras]] so not to alert authorities that it is automated) into the desert. The party notices something bizarre in the desert sands... a corpse with a stone on it! The stone... is... very special. 

Suddenly, the stone begins to talk! [[Gogol]] immediately recognizes this voice to be that of a rebel named [[Gustav]], the Gun, who was once a feral mountain man who helped the [[Snjero United Nations ]]with his pet bear, [[Flea]]. However, when he changed sides, they trapped his soul in a stone... someone must've stolen this stone, and died trying to reach the rebel base. Being a friend of [[Gogol]]'s, they take the stone containing Gun with them. Despite having to fight off a sand worm, the party makes their way through the desert to the city of [[Corlah]]. 

Gogol stops the wagon to rest in [[Corlah]] for the evening. The party is free to explore the desert town. Gun is put on a necklace and worn by Beth. In the market, [[Beth]] learns that the soul stone [[Gustav|Gun]] is trapped in is a soul stone, and that the [[Snjero]] government will often use these soul stones to power their Roja Bots, but usually the stone is quiet and is used as an energy source. [[Gustav|Gun]] is different, as he appears to have retained his identity in the stone. [[Beth]] is warned to not let government officials know she has him...

At the same time, [[Eira]] gets drunk at the mead hall, and transforms into a swarm of bees, so she decides to go to the apiary. [[Viktor]] decides to rest at the [[Black Hand]] tent where he speaks with the local clergy. While he rests, Gun and Beth meet [[Eira]] at the apiary, where the group learns something strange is going on at the closed down mine... [[Viktor]] leans the same news and all decide to go on their own to the same location... however, [[Viktor]] drinks a drug known as [[Chevrik Drink]]... oh boy.... 

The party all arrives at the closed mine, [[Viktor]] high out of his mind. They find the mine infested with large desert spiders. Between [[Viktor]]s trip and [[Gustav|Gun]] speaking with the spiders, they all become friendly, and the spiders let the party pass into the mine unharmed, [[Viktor]] riding a frigging spider!!! 

Once deep in the mine, the party discovers another bizarre room similar to the one found by [[Viktor]] and [[Eira]].. this time, a vampire comes out of the coffin to meet them...

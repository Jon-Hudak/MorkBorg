Our adventurers all started on their own separate paths, each with their own missions. 

## [[Eira]]: 
[[Eira]] is on the run from the government for blowing up a small naval port in[[ Odinski ]]due to the enslavement of flame elementals to power their ships. Desperate to escape, she fled North to [[Snaefeljoko]] where she met an old contact who informed her the flame elementals were taken from the reserve. She attempted a break in to free them. She managed to sneak in, but the flame elementals were so oppressed they viewed her as an enemy rather than their rescuer. Her help was in vain as they burned her alive.... as she died, she saw that not only were they enslaving them for ships, but planned to use them as bombs as she saw evidence of this before passing on. 

## [[Beth]] and [[Lil Beth]]: 
[[Beth]] was on a tour to promote her music with her little companion, so when visiting [[Snjero]] they went to the city of party and music. Her performance was so powerful that it inspired rebellion, and a [[Gogol|rebel leader]], later to be revealed to be the king of [[Jarla]] before the [[Snjero United Nations]] took over, [[Gogol]], took interest. He invited her to talk in the main tent of the[[NPCs/The Holy Family/Holy Family]]. While in the tent, an orcish war ship landed on the shore and stormed the festival. As the snjero guards fell, the rebellion army appeared to counterstrike the orcs. In the fight, [[Gogol]] rescued [[Beth]] and [[Lil Beth]], taking them into a rebel transport automated wagon. She managed to miss a cannon shot that seemed destined to kill her. They began to drive off to the rebel base as the resistance fought off the orcs. 

## [[Ragnar]]: 
The Goliath set off to the capital to Assassinate [[General Kerstad]] based on a letter that was mysteriously left in front of him. Wanting acceptance from a new government, and never fearing a challenge, he took on the contract. He first scoped the area at the hospital. He went to the Masha hotel to find the general where the letter said. He found several guards in the main hotel, who after a rough fight, he made it to the courtyard. He soon realized the letter was a fake. The general planned to use Ragnar as a symbol to show how to crush a rebellion in single combat. Whether it was spiked wine, or simply the general was that good... [[Ragnar]] fell. As he lay dying, he heard an explosion, and the troops were heard rushing to the [[NPCs/The Holy Family/Holy Family]].

## [[Viktor]]: 
[[Viktor]] was sent by the [[Black Hand]] to investigate strange deaths on the coast of [[Chard]] in [[Snaefeljoko]]. Upon arrival at the guard post, he was directed to the bodies up along the coast. Viktor found the body, and to his surprise, he found the death was caused by a vampire, which were thought to be eradicated by the [[Black Hand]] in [[Snjero]] years ago. He managed to kill one, and before the other surprise attacked him, it fell dead by a gunshot from [[Billy Graves]].. aka the Hat Man, a known enemy of [[Snjero]]. Hat Man engaged in a conversation with Viktor, but comfort was ended by a loud explosion, as [[Viktor]] realized [[Billy Graves|Hat Man]] had exploded the [[NPCs/The Holy Family/Holy Family]] Temple in the area with some sort of bomb... He challenged the [[Billy Graves|Hat Man]], but fell to his bullet... 

The Aftermath: 
The three dead found themselves aboard the ship to the afterlife.. where they met the Pilgrim, who informed them that they would not go to the Village or the Afterlife. Rather... they were to meet the[[NPCs/The Holy Family/Holy Family]]. Inside the ship, they feasted and talked. While on the ship, the soul of [[Beth]] was summoned via Astral projection to join the feast. [[Father]] explained that these four have seen something across all three countries that seemed like an attack on the [[NPCs/The Holy Family/Holy Family]].. at the same time, three separate temples were destroyed. Father believed that this was an organized strike, likely form within the government from someone corrupt. What bothered the Family was that they were unable to see the cause of the attack. It was shrouded from the omnipotent vision... things were not happening in the way they always did. For example, [[Father]] saw [[Beth]] die, but the order of things were off and she miraculously lived. The family made a deal with the party: they were to find the culprit who destroyed the temples.. in return, the three who died would live again. [[The Son]] made an additional agreement to [[Eira]] to help her stop the fire elemental cruelty. [[The Mother]] promised [[Beth]] great fame for her music if she helped. After the feast, the three dead were revived and awoke in the [[Black Hand]] Temple. Beth's soul returned to her body and she awoke in the cart with [[Gogol]]. Upon instruction of the Family, they were all to meet at the abandoned [[Machina]] ship, the home of the rebellion, because if the threat came from inside the government, only people truly against the government would be safe to work with. The rebels were also known to be very devout to the Family... So it is likely they will rally to help. Will the three revived souls manage to make it to the [[Machina]]? Will [[Beth]] and the [[Gogol|secret king]] meet them there? Find out in the next log!

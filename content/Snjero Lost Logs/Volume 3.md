This Log tells what happened to [[Ragnar]] while [[Eira]] and [[Viktor]] went on a quest.

Summoning sickness takes a toll on the body, and often can leave a person out of commission for days on end. [[Ragnar]] was special... 

The first night of summoning sickness left [[Ragnar]] in a deep sleep, so deep, that he had a prophetic dream. He was greeted by [[Father]] aboard the ship, who said since he just came back from the dead that he can still commune with him for a short time. He scolded [[Ragnar]] for being weak enough to fall to summoning sickness, and that he should wake up and get to training! His comrades will be back soon and he needs to be better than he is.

The next day, [[Ragnar]] awoke, hours after [[Eira]] and [[Viktor]] left for [[Utton]]. He was still groggy, but recovered exceptionally faster than any normal being would. He met [[Aleksi]] for a meal and the two talked. He told [[Aleksi]] that [[Father]] came to him in a dream, and that he must train. [[Aleksi]] then agreed to train him in combat. 

The two went to the courtyard and began to fight, no fear of death as [[Aleksi]] was certain if one of them were to die, [[Father]] would send them back to the temple. At first, [[Aleksi]] was besting [[Ragnar]] in combat, but after some time, the summoning sickness finally left his body, and [[Ragnar]] won the fight, beheading [[Aleksi]]! [[Ragnar]] himself was well beaten, bleeding severely, he passed out. He woke up the next day, with his wounds healed from potions, and walked to the courtyard where he saw [[Aleksi]]'s headless body lying. 

As he looked at the corpse, a perfect copy of [[Aleksi]] came behind him and laughed at how crazy reincarnation is from the [[NPCs/The Holy Family/Holy Family|Family]]. He gave [[Ragnar]] some money, and encouraged him to spend some time enjoying a [[Snjero]] town while he waits for [[Eira]] and [[Viktor]] to return. 

[[Ragnar]] explored the shopping center, where he bought gifts for his new friends and found a mysterious black stick. The shop owner explained that there is a [[Snjero]] legend that beyond the large border wall, sits two magical trees, one of life and one of death, and that this branch came from one of those trees. [[Ragnar]] was enamored by the story, and wanted to learn more. He continued to explore the town and found himself at [[Masha's Supply Shop]]. 

[[Masha]] appeared to be an interesting woman, who sensed Ragnar was not lawful, revealing that she knew many secrets of [[Snjero]]. Since her goods were sold out from [[Eira]] and [[Viktor]], she offered him information for his coin instead. [[Masha]] told Ragnar about how the wall to the[[ Great Merge wall]] was heavily guarded, but what the guards failed to realize was that while all their attention was focused on the wall, they did not think anyone would go under it... she revealed a secret tunnel existed in the shady part of town, so [[Ragnar]] went to the shady part. 

At the tavern, he befriended the bar keep, who revealed he supported the resistance. He told [[Ragnar]] that the entrance lay in the basement of an abandoned house in this part of town. With the people of this neighborhood being in an uproar from the emperor's announcement that [[Ragnar]] learned of, he took advantage of the chaos and snuck into the abandoned home.

Inside the home, he found a hidden basement with a very deep cave. Going in, he took damage as he fell through a pit, but he remained strong... he was met by a large shadowy figure who scared him by making him relive every event that made him feel fear.... the shock would kill most adventurers.. but somehow... [[Ragnar]] survived. 

Impressed, the shadowy figure took him inside the [[Great Merge Wall]] where he saw a world almost incomprehensible.... The shadowy figure was now revealed, a large, demonic looking Tiefling, known as [[Derrick]], the God of Fear.. who's origin was much older than the Holy Family... he gifted him a stick from the tree of life, and revealed some hidden knowledge as a sign of respect... after talking. Ragnar blinked, he was back in the abandoned house with the hidden entrance once again covered.

He started walking back to the [[NPCs/The Holy Family/Holy Family]] temple, who he saw [[Eira]] and [[Viktor]]. Before he was close enough to talk, he saw [[Aleksi]] shoot a glass amulet in a box, and right when he was in ear shot, he heard [[Aleksi]] say...

"He's back."

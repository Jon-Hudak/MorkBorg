Disguised man in full plate with a greatsword, looking to learn more about the rebel movement. 

His disguise was quickly sussed out by the party, when they learned he was in fact Ratimir Grozny, the fearsome general of the Snjero army, known for his merciless demeanor, bordering on sadistic.
